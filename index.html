How it works

Three habits. The whole back office.

This is the real app with a real electrician's books in it. Nothing staged.

01

Say it.

When your hands are full, talk. Say who the job was for and what it cost. PicoTally writes the invoice with your rates, prices the materials from your past jobs, puts your logo on it, and has it ready to send before the tools are packed. Change your mind out loud and watch it happen on the screen.

“Make it no tax.” “Add a 25 percent markup.” “Send it to Greg.”

Hey Pico is part of Pro Max. Everything also works by tap.

02

Snap it.

Photograph the receipt at the counter. PicoTally reads every line, keeps the business items, sets the personal ones aside, and files each one under a category your accountant will recognize. The shoebox never happens, and at filing time it's one clean report instead of a bag of paper.

03

Ask it.

Who owes me money? How much did I spend on materials this month? Ask in plain English and get names and dollar amounts from your actual books, not a guess. Drafts you forgot, quotes still waiting, invoices overdue and by how many days.

“Who owes me the most?” “What did I make this month?”

Straight answers

What people ask before they sign up.

Is this built for my trade?

If you send invoices and buy materials, yes. Electricians, plumbers, HVAC, handymen, landscapers, painters, and every one-person shop in between. If you run a crew with an office manager, you'll outgrow it, and that's fine.

Do I have to talk to it?

No. Everything works by tap, on the phone and on the desktop. Pro includes the chat assistant. The hands-free "Hey Pico" voice is the Pro Max plan, for when your hands are full or you're driving.

Which plan do I need?

Pro if you want the paperwork done: invoices, receipts, expenses, the tax-time report, and the chat assistant. Pro Max if you want to do it by voice from the truck. Both start with a free month, and you can switch.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Write invoices, snap receipts and log expenses in a basement with no bars. Everything saves on the device and syncs the moment you're back in coverage.

What happens to my stuff if I quit?

You export everything and you keep it: jobs, expenses, clients and payments. Cancel whenever you like, with nobody trying to talk you out of it.

Will it do my taxes?

It gets you ready for them. Every deductible expense is tracked and categorized as you go, and at filing time you hand yourself or your accountant one clean report. It does not file for you, and it will not pretend to give you tax advice.

Is my customer list safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and on the iPhone the local database is encrypted with a key held in the device Keychain. We don't sell data, we don't run ads against it, and we have no business reason to look at your books.
