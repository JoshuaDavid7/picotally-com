Picotally

Invoicing, expenses, and quotes for tradespeople — all from your phone.

What it does

  • Scan a receipt with your camera and Picotally itemises it for you.
  • Speak a customer's name and Picotally drafts the invoice.
  • Send a PDF with one tap. Add an online payment link so customers can pay you faster.
  • Everything works offline. Syncs when you're back on signal.
