Straight answers

What people ask me before they sign up.

Can I actually use it today?

Yes — open it in any browser on a laptop or desktop and you are working in a couple of minutes. The iPhone app is on its way to the App Store, and Android is in the works. Everything you do on one shows up on the others.

Is this built for my trade?

If you send invoices and buy materials, yes. Electricians, plumbers, HVAC, handymen, landscapers, painters, remodelers, and every one-person shop in between. It is built for the person who runs the whole business and does their own books.

Do I have to talk to it?

No. Everything works by clicking and typing, the same as any other app. The voice is there for when your hands are full or you are covered in something, which for most of us is most of the day.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Write invoices, snap receipts and log expenses in a basement with no bars. Everything saves on the device and syncs itself the moment you are back in coverage.

What happens to my stuff if I quit?

You export everything and you keep it — jobs, expenses, clients and payments. Your books are yours. Cancel whenever you like, with nobody trying to talk you out of it.

Will it do my taxes?

It gets you ready for them. Every deductible expense is tracked and categorised as you go, and at filing time you hand yourself or your accountant one clean report. It does not file for you, and it will not pretend to give you tax advice.

Is my customer list safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and on the iPhone the local database is encrypted with a key held in the device Keychain. We do not sell data, we do not run ads against it, and we have no business reason to look at your books.
